# 0.1.5.2

Released September 17, 2026.

## Fixes

- Fixed errors handling items with custom damage types, jukebox songs, and instruments on Paper.
- Fixed contents, amounts, and slot positions changing inside shulker boxes and bundles, and fixed loaded crossbow projectiles.
- Fixed item attributes, equipment properties, tool rules, and custom data changing when items are recreated.
- Fixed item compatibility errors caused by differences between Paper versions.
- Fixed incorrect currency removal from nested containers and partial withdrawals returning container currency items.
- Prevented failed inventory operations from leaving partially added or removed currency, or updating the recorded inventory balance.
